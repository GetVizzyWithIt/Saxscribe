# ADR-0009: Proceed with data on hand; Filosax and MedleyDB are later rounds

- **Date**: 2026-11-02
- **Status**: Accepted
- **Decided by**: Austin

## Context
Filosax (the requirements' primary solo set and v1 gate) and MedleyDB-Pitch
need access approval, which was requested and is pending.

## Decision
Don't wait. Build and measure with the ~125 GB already on the Mac (Omnibook,
URMP, Bach10, ChoraleBricks and others). Treat datasets that arrive later as
future training iterations.

## Consequences
- v1 gate 1 (solo targets on Filosax test split) can't be measured yet.
- The CREPE Notes comparison stays unresolved until Filosax arrives.
